## 2. Findings, ranked by listener harm

### F1 — Two planned stages switch themselves on: S13 correction "auto-load" and S18 "HRTF default-on" break "a fresh launch plays the file as recorded"
- **Items:** S13 (`sprint-plan.md:83` "device-correction EQ auto-load by identified device"); US-TON-02 (`backlog.md:537` "automatically apply a frequency response correction … right out of the box"); Journey 2.4 Step 2 (`docs/product/user-journeys.md:142-145` "If no profile exists → loads generic profile for the device category"); S18 / US-SPAT-01 (`backlog.md:1336` "HRTF is default-on for any device classified as headphones"); FD-2 (`council README.md:183`); the code's faithful defaults (`ListeningSettings.swift:4-9`; `EQMigration.swift:40-41`).
- **Type:** contradiction.
- **What the listener experiences:** a new install with the XM4 connected is corrected (and, after S18, binauralised) before they touch anything. The hero chip says only "ENHANCED" — it names Intensity and crossfeed, nothing else (`AdaptiveSound/UI/NowPlaying/HeroBand.swift:110-115,154-159`) — so the honesty promise fails too: processing plays and nothing on screen says which. The docs themselves disagree on whether correction has a switch (three readings in the inventory row).
- **Severity:** must resolve before S13.
- **Recommendation:** correction and spatial are opt-in per device and off on a fresh install; "auto-load" means *offer and remember* (US-DEVICE-04's own wording: "app offers to auto-load; manual switch also available", `backlog.md:777`), never *apply*; and every stage that plays gets a hero chip the way "XF:" does. Alternatives: auto-apply with a 6-second "Undo" toast (the "Bring back edits" pattern); or auto-apply only on a device the listener already chose correction for (that is opt-in).

### F2 — Intensity (S17) returns as one knob over three stages the listener will already switch separately, and its row contradicts a standing founder decision on crossfeed
- **Items:** S17 (`sprint-plan.md:112` "Map intensity 0→1 across loudness-comp + clarity (+ crossfeed)"; `roadmap.md:150`); the 2026-10-14 decision that crossfeed is "an output stage of its own … at its preset's full strength whatever Intensity is" (`docs/product/known-issues.md:748-751`; code `DSPKernel.cpp:321-324`); S14-lite's own control ("the loudness button", `sprint-plan.md:84`); US-ADAPT-05 ("Adaptation Strength … while keeping my manual EQ corrections … At 100% (default)", `backlog.md:690-699`); FR-REIMAGINE-02 ("At 0%, … all transformation stages shall be bypassed", `requirements.md:665-666`); the kept UI copy "Off ↔ Full Blend" / "0 % — Reimagine off (your EQ still plays)" (`InspectorColumn.swift:119,124-131`).
- **Type:** contradiction.
- **What the listener experiences:** they turned Loudness on in R2. S17 lands with Intensity 0 (FD-2) — does their Loudness switch go dark? If Intensity 0 "bypasses all transformation stages", their own switch is silently overridden; if not, Intensity 0 is no longer "faithful". The same question for Clarity. And the row still lists crossfeed, which the founder already took out of the blend. Two stories give two defaults (0 vs 100 %).
- **Severity:** must resolve before S17; the decision is cheaper taken before S14 ships its switch.
- **Recommendation:** decide now that Intensity is the *strength of the automatic stages only* (Clarity, and S14's adaptive half when it exists) — it never scales a switch the listener set by hand (Loudness, crossfeed, EQ, correction); drop "(+ crossfeed)" from S17; amend FR-REIMAGINE-02 to "0 = nothing automatic". Alternatives: (a) S14 ships *no* switch and Intensity is the only way to get loudness compensation (then "the loudness button" wording goes); (b) keep Intensity hidden for good and give each stage its own control — the simplest story, at the cost of the "one knob" thesis.

### F3 — When Intensity comes back it re-enables the session-long leveller FD-2 removed, on top of R1.1-B's Leveling — and the docs place Leveling's engine work in two different sprints
- **Items:** the blend path still fades the normaliser in ("Loudness on the blend, its contribution faded toward 0 dB as r → 0", `DSPKernel.cpp:313-319`; `LoudnessParams.enabled = 1`, `TargetState.h:60-66`); C-38 ("the normaliser is a session-long AGC … the real fix is M (S14 slice)", `stage5-architect.md:108`); US-R1F-10 (a *static* gain from the existing analysis, R1.1-B, `backlog.md:1756-1763`); the architect's slice catalogue putting "C-38 (static per-track/album gain + true-peak cap)" in **W5 at S12/S13** (`stage5-architect.md:334`), not R1.1-B.
- **Type:** contradiction (docs vs docs) and overlap (two levellers).
- **What the listener experiences:** in R3 they set Leveling = Album, then raise Intensity for Clarity, and the old AGC comes back underneath: make-up gain carrying across tracks, quiet tracks gated out — double leveling, and a level change from a knob that is supposed to change sound, not loudness (C-40).
- **Severity:** must resolve before S17; should before R1.1-B (so the leveling story removes the old leveller rather than sitting beside it).
- **Recommendation:** R1.1-B's Leveling story replaces the kernel's `LoudnessModule` make-up with the static leveling gain on *every* path (so the blend path has no leveller of its own), and S17's blend is level-neutral (the correlation-compensated blend of C-40). Alternatives: leave the normaliser in the kernel but hard-wire `enabled = 0` whenever Leveling is Off (a flag the UI story must mention).

### F4 — After R2 the listener has five things that change the level, three names that mean the same thing to different people, and no place that shows the sum
- **Items:** Master Gain in the inspector; Leveling "a setting" with no home (`backlog.md:1758`); loudness compensation with no home and two caps (`sprint-plan.md:84` +12 dB; `requirements.md:354` +6/+4 dB; `backlog.md:556` "S14 must settle one set"); the EQ's hidden neutral gain (decision 26; "There is no Headroom readout anywhere", `ui-plan.md:249`); the limiter (no UI); the Settings tab today holds only Output Device, Pure Mode, Formats (`AdaptiveSound/UI/Tabs/SettingsTabView.swift:18-20`); decision 31 renamed the "Loudness" preset to "Bass & Treble" to avoid exactly this clash (`s10-8-glass-sweep-plan.md:127-128`).
- **What other players taught the listener:** Roon calls it **Volume Leveling** — "Off", "Auto", "Track", "Album", "Roon targets a final loudness of -14 LUFS", "the audio signal is multiplied by a constant gain value" ([Roon KB](https://help.roonlabs.com/portal/en/kb/articles/volume-leveling)); Apple calls it **Sound Check** — "Set all songs and music videos to always play at the same volume" ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-bh/guide/music/musa3fedf052)); Spotify calls it **Normalize volume** with "Loud / Normal / Quiet" ([Spotify](https://support.spotify.com/article/volume-normalization/)). Hi-fi receivers call the ISO 226 tilt "Loudness". So "Leveling" and "Loudness" are, to a Spotify listener, the same feature with a level picker, and to a receiver owner "Loudness" is a bass boost.
- **Type:** complication.
- **What the listener experiences:** at night they want bass back at low volume, turn on "Leveling", and the album gets *quieter*; or they want tracks even and turn on "Loudness", and the tone changes instead. Nothing reads "you are 9 dB under the file right now".
- **Severity:** should resolve naming before R1.1-B; must give the controls one home before S14.
- **Recommendation:** one "Level" card (Settings, or the inspector — one place) holding, in signal order, Master Gain, "Even out track levels: Off / Album / Track", and "Keep bass and treble at low volume" — plain words, not "Loudness"; one caption line under it reads the net gain the app is applying now; the limiter and the EQ's neutral gain stay invisible. Decide the cap once (recommend FR-TONAL-03's +6/+4 dB — the listener-safe pair — and let the +12 dB trust clamp be the ceiling, not the target). Alternatives: keep the names and caption each with what it does to the level; or adopt Roon's names outright, since the founder's audience reads Roon.

### F5 — There is no story that forces the founder's own litmus test ("more transparent on an A/B") before S14, S16 or S18 ship; S16 is the item most likely to fail it
- **Items:** S15/S16 rows ("A/B vs bypass", no pass bar, `sprint-plan.md:110-111`); the council's conditions ("S15's exit criterion should be the same kind of test … If it doesn't win, S16 is cut", `docs/reviews/2026-10-council/working/product.md:230-233`; "Clarity **only if it wins a blind test**", `:211-213`) that reached no story; SPIKE-LOUDNESS-COMP-TUNING's "MUSHRA-style listening test, 10+ listeners" (`backlog.md:2205`) — not something one founder can run; US-TON-03's AC "audibly noticeable but not artificial or 'pumpy'" (`requirements.md:358`) with no test; US-SPAT-01's AC "naive listener ABX test shows audible spatial difference vs. bypass" (`backlog.md:1332`) — proves *difference*, not *preference*, the wrong bar; the adaptive-tone study's finding "No published listening test shows automatic EQ beating an untouched finished master" (`docs/design/adaptive-tone/README.md:50`) and its ready-made blind ladder (`:221-241`, Stage 1: SPRT, about 23 trials, cap 60).
- **Type:** complication.
- **What the listener experiences:** a stage ships because it measured right, and the founder, who prefers faithful sound (`product.md:201-205`), stops using Enhanced — the thesis never meets its one user.
- **Severity:** must resolve before S15 starts (the exit bar) and before S14 ships (one evening's blind test on the founder's gear).
- **Recommendation:** add one story — "before any processing stage ships, the founder runs a blind, level-matched A/B on their own headphones and soundbar (the adaptive-tone §6 Stage 1 protocol); a stage that does not win ships off and hidden" — and make it S14's, S16's and S18's last acceptance line. Alternatives: ship every stage off by default and skip the test (cheaper; S16/S18 then carry no proof of value); or make the test the demo-day event of each sprint.

### F6 — "Compare" will mean three different things after S12/S13/S16, and S13's correction layer makes today's meaning the wrong comparison
- **Items:** Compare = "hear the song without the EQ, at matched loudness … The engine needs only On/Off" (`EQCompareButton.swift:5`; decision 28, `s10-8-glass-sweep-plan.md:115-117`); S12 "whether S12 still owes a whole-chain A/B is open" (`sprint-plan.md:82`); US-PROF-02 (a whole-enhancement, LUFS-matched, *bit-transparent* bypass, `backlog.md:1445-1458`); S13 "a correction layer and the user's taste on one graph" (`sprint-plan.md:83`); S16 "A/B vs bypass" (`sprint-plan.md:111`); the market: "correction … sits *under* your taste curve" (`eq-market-research.md:178`); loudness compensation exempt from the neutral gain (W5-min, `stage5-architect.md:57,334`).
- **Type:** overlap.
- **What the listener experiences:** after S13 they hold Compare to judge their Warm curve and hear uncorrected headphones — taste-vs-raw, not taste-vs-corrected. After S14, if Compare removes the compensation tilt too, "matched loudness" is no longer true (the tilt is not neutral by design). After S16 a second A/B appears for Clarity. Which button, which layers, is never said.
- **Severity:** must resolve before S13; should before S12.
- **Recommendation:** Compare keeps exactly one meaning — "without *my* EQ" — and leaves correction, leveling and compensation in place; S12 adds a separate, latching **Bypass all processing** (US-PROF-02, bit-transparent, matched loudness) that S16 reuses; every other layer has an on/off, not its own Compare. Alternatives: Compare opens a small menu ("Without: my EQ / correction / Clarity / everything").

### F7 — Three per-device memories (EQ pins, S13 profiles, a global PEQ) with no rule for which wins; AutoEq's own preamp meets the EQ's neutral gain
- **Items:** pins: one slot per device UID with a model fallback (`EQStore.swift:46-57`), "Used on … · same model" (`EQOutputPinButton.swift:45`), unpin overwrites the shared EQ with no undo (KI-EQ-01, `EQStore.swift:112-115`; `known-issues.md:283-289`); S13 profiles: "Profile stores: EQ bands, spatialization settings, dynamics settings, Adaptation Strength, linked device ID … app offers to auto-load" (`backlog.md:777-778`) and "Toggle persists per profile" (`backlog.md:545`); S12 PEQ "alongside the 31-band graphic" with no slot story (`sprint-plan.md:82`); AutoEq files "carry their own negative preamp" (`eq-market-research.md:70-71`) while the EQ replaced its preamp with a loudness-neutral gain (decision 26).
- **Type:** complication.
- **What the listener experiences:** the XM4 has a pinned "Warm · edited" EQ, then a profile "Night — Sony" with its own bands and correction toggle, plus a PEQ that may be global; plugging in loads which? An imported AutoEq curve with "Preamp: −6.2 dB" either ignores the line (louder than its author meant; the limiter works) or applies it on top of the neutral gain (6 dB too quiet) — either way "what you see is what plays" is gone.
- **Severity:** should resolve before S12 (where the PEQ lives), must before S13.
- **Recommendation:** the existing slot (`EQState`) is the only per-output memory: extend it with the PEQ bands and the correction choice (profile id + on/off), and "export a profile" becomes "export this output's slot"; on AutoEq import drop the preamp line and say so in the sheet ("level is matched automatically"). Fix KI-EQ-01 in the same change, since profiles will inherit it. Alternatives: build profiles as the superset and migrate pins into them (riskier for the listener's edits).

### F8 — S14-lite's loudness button follows Master Gain, not the volume the founder actually turns
- **Items:** S14-lite: "The adaptive half — the system volume observed, engine events — waits for W2" (`sprint-plan.md:84`; `stage5-architect.md:426`); US-TON-03's AC "Compensation updates on every volume change event" (`backlog.md:560`); Journey 2.3 Step 4 "User changes volume (system slider or in-app)" (`user-journeys.md:108-112`); code: on Enhanced Master Gain is the app's own gain (`+MasterGain.swift:22-25`) and the Mac's volume is a separate thing; on Bluetooth the volume is device-side; on HDMI it is "Set on device" (`+MasterGain.swift:67`). The founder's own outputs are Bluetooth headphones and an HDMI soundbar.
- **Type:** contradiction between the story's AC and the -lite scope.
- **What the listener experiences:** they turn the XM4 down with the volume keys at night and the loudness button does nothing; they drag Master Gain down and it works; nothing says which volume counts.
- **Severity:** must resolve before S14 (in the story and the copy).
- **Recommendation:** make it explicit: the S14-lite switch's caption says "follows Master Gain", the Master Gain readout shows the compensation it is driving ("−12 dB · bass +4 dB"), and the device-volume input is written into the story as the S14-adaptive half. Alternative: read the device volume now through the HAL seam that already exists for Pure (`MasterGainOnPure.swift:59-62` `DeviceVolumeControl.read()`) — the docs tie it to W2, but the level *read* needs no engine events (inferred).

### F9 — Three answers to "how do I hear this on headphones" — the fold (R1.1-B), crossfeed (shipped), BRIR (S18) — and no story says how many switches, or which wins
- **Items:** US-R1F-08 names no control (`backlog.md:1732-1738`); crossfeed's inspector section (`InspectorColumn.swift:143-186`); US-SPAT-01 "default-on for headphones" (`backlog.md:1336`), US-SPAT-03 room "off by default" (`backlog.md:1369`); the kernel's own warning: "The control layer (Realizer/VM) must never enable both … When S18 BRIR ships, treat `crossfeed.enabled && brir.active` as a control-layer bug and deterministically prefer one (document which then)" (`TargetState.h:102-105`); FR-SPAT-03 "crossfeed … is largely subsumed by the BRIR path" (`requirements.md:294-295`); S4 design "the reverb amount maps to the existing Reimagine/intensity dial" (`05-sprint-5b-s4-binaural-design.md:76-79`); a 5.1 file asked for in Pure plays Enhanced ("This track has a different number of channels than your device, so Enhanced is playing it", `PureModeReason.swift:105-106`) and will get the fold.
- **Type:** overlap.
- **What the listener experiences:** a "Headphones" section with Crossfeed on; S18 adds spatial, on by default; both run (the kernel calls that a bug); the "XF" chip can't tell them apart; a Pure listener's surround album plays through a matrix and a ceiling they never chose, with only "Pure off: channels" to explain it.
- **Severity:** should resolve before S18; the Pure wording for the fold belongs in R1.1-B.
- **Recommendation:** one "Headphones" control with three positions — Off / Crossfeed / Spatial (room amount under Spatial) — exclusive by construction; the fold has no control but a hero chip ("5.1 → stereo") and the Pure reason reads "…so Enhanced plays it, folded to stereo". Alternatives: two switches that grey each other out.

### F10 — Everything planned is silently off in Pure, and only the EQ says so
- **Items:** the EQ strip (`EQGraphStrip.swift:73-80`), the Settings row (`SignalPathStatus.swift:96-98`), Master Gain's tooltip (`+MasterGain.swift:98`) all speak; crossfeed's switch stays enabled on Pure with headphones (`InspectorColumn.swift:149,172-175`) and only the chip disappears (`SignalPathInfo.swift:107-109`); US-R1F-10, US-TON-03, US-TON-02, US-PERC-03, US-SPAT-01 say nothing about Pure.
- **Type:** complication.
- **What the listener experiences:** Pure on, they flip Leveling to Album and nothing happens; flip Loudness, nothing; turn correction on, nothing — and no word why, five times over.
- **Severity:** should resolve — one rule in R1.1-B, reused by every later story.
- **Recommendation:** one shared "Not in Pure Mode" row state (the pattern of "Connect headphones to enable.", `InspectorColumn.swift:161`) applied to Leveling, Loudness, Correction, Clarity, Spatial, and crossfeed too; the Pure card lists what is being skipped ("Skipped in Pure: EQ, Leveling, Loudness"). Alternative: hide the controls in Pure (worse: the listener can't see what they'll get back).

### F11 — The built-in EQ preset "Clarity" and the S16 feature "Clarity" share a name — the "Loudness" preset was renamed for exactly this
- **Items:** `EQBuiltInPreset.swift:29,54,67` (`case clarity = "Clarity"`, displayName "Clarity"); decision 31 (`s10-8-glass-sweep-plan.md:127-128`: "Loudness" → "Bass & Treble" "because it would clash with S14's loudness compensation"); S16 (`sprint-plan.md:111`; `roadmap.md:149`); the council's own test name "Clarity vs the static 'Clarity' preset" (`product.md:232`).
- **Type:** contradiction (naming).
- **What the listener experiences:** "Turn on Clarity" — the preset or the engine? The output toast "AirPods Pro · Clarity" after S16 is ambiguous; a blind test report "Clarity vs Clarity" cannot be written.
- **Severity:** should resolve before S15 (rename the display name now; the stored id stays, as decision 31 did).
- **Recommendation:** rename the preset's `displayName` ("Detail" or "Bright"), stored id unchanged. Alternative: name the S16 feature something else ("Unmask") and keep the preset.

### F12 — Master Gain on Pure moves the Mac's volume, and gives it back at moments the listener didn't choose
- **Items:** `MasterGainOnPure.swift:7-23` (entering Pure lowers the device; a volume-key press is read as "the Mac's own volume with Master Gain's cut still on top"; leaving Pure "puts the anchor back"; Pure is left by "a stop that ends playback (Stop, the end of the queue, the queue cleared, quit)"); KI-SET-03's closing note "'Silent' still sets the device to its minimum, which on some devices is not silence" (`known-issues.md:897`). *The task mentioned a known issue about the volume HUD; I found none — grep "HUD" across `docs/` hits only `docs/sprints/s9-browse-search-ui-design.md:296` (a toast idiom).*
- **Type:** complication (honest and reasoned, but surprising).
- **What the listener experiences:** in Pure they press the volume keys; macOS shows its HUD at one level, the app's Master Gain slider stays where it was (by design); two readouts, one level. At the end of the album the Mac's volume comes back up on its own. On Enhanced afterwards, Master Gain is the app's gain again — the same slider, a different thing.
- **Severity:** note; the design is shipped and reasoned. The gap is that nothing in the app shows the device level while Pure holds it, and the give-back is silent.
- **Recommendation:** on Pure the readout shows both ("−6 dB · device at −18 dB"), the give-back raises a toast ("Volume restored"), and the FD-5 hardware check lists "volume keys during Pure, then end of queue". Alternative: give back only on an explicit leave (Pure off, device change), not at end of queue.
