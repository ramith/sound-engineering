**AdaptiveSound 1.1.0-alpha** (R1.1) plays your music as it was recorded by default. It adds leveling, so songs
and albums sit at an even loudness, and folds surround music properly to headphones and stereo speakers. It also
fixes most of 1.0's known issues. It is still an early build: expect rough edges, and please report what you find.

## Requirements
- macOS 26 (Tahoe) on Apple Silicon.
- Optional: [FFmpeg](https://formulae.brew.sh/formula/ffmpeg) via Homebrew (`brew install ffmpeg`), for formats
  Apple's decoders don't cover. It is now off until you turn it on in **Settings › Formats**.

## Install
1. Download `AdaptiveSound-1.1.0-alpha-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag **AdaptiveSound** to **Applications**.
2. This alpha is **not notarized by Apple yet**, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open **System Settings ›
   Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to AdaptiveSound. You only do this once.
   - Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AdaptiveSound.app`
3. **Coming from 1.0.0-alpha:** 1.1 upgrades your library the first time it opens it, and keeps your play counts,
   loved songs, ratings and playlists. The upgrade is one way: 1.0.0-alpha can no longer open the library afterwards.

## Verify the download
These files were built by GitHub Actions from this release's tag, not on a personal machine, and each one carries a
signed build-provenance attestation (Sigstore). To check one:
```
gh attestation verify AdaptiveSound-1.1.0-alpha-macos-arm64.dmg --repo ramith/sound-engineering
```
`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of each file.

## What's new since 1.0.0-alpha
**The sound**
- **A faithful default.** A fresh install plays the music as recorded, with the EQ off and leveling off. Your settings
  are kept across launches: Pure, Master Gain, crossfeed, shuffle and repeat, and the output device you chose.
- **Leveling** (Settings › Leveling: Off, Track, Album or Auto).
  - Songs play at an even −18 LUFS, so a loud modern master and a quiet old recording sit together without reaching
    for the volume.
  - **Album** keeps an album's own quiet and loud moments, and **Auto** does that whenever the next song is from the
    same album.
  - The level never changes inside a song.
  - The app measures the songs coming up in the background. A song not measured yet plays a little quieter the
    first time, to be safe.
- **Surround music on headphones and stereo speakers.** A 5.1 or 7.1 track is folded to stereo by the broadcast
  standard (ITU-R BS.775), so you hear the centre and surrounds too. The LFE is included only when it carries bass of
  its own. Channels are placed by the file's own labels, at every sample rate. A "5.1 → stereo" badge shows it on
  Now Playing.
- **Better conversion and joins.**
  - Apple's top-quality (Mastering) sample-rate converter is now used, flat to 20 kHz.
  - Gapless albums at a rate other than 48 kHz join seamlessly.
  - Dolby (AC-3 / E-AC-3) tracks and the last song of the queue play to their last frame.
- **Crossfeed** works on its own and follows the bs2b reference. It applies to headphones in the Mac's own jack, not
  to AirPlay speakers.
- **The limiter** recovers from a single peak in about 150 ms (it took about 1 s).
- **Pure Mode:**
  - Master Gain now turns your device's own volume down, so playback stays bit-perfect.
  - When Pure can't play a track exactly, a short reason shows, such as "Pure off: Bluetooth".
  - In Pure, the sound controls are inactive and say "Off in bit-perfect mode", and VoiceOver says so too.
  - "Leave Pure" switches back to Enhanced from any of them.

**Playing and the queue**
- **Shuffle** plays every song once per pass, and Previous goes back through what you heard.
- **The queue survives a crash or force-quit:** it comes back paused, on the same song, within a few seconds of where
  you were. A scrub is saved at once.
- **Open files from outside your library** with Open With, by dropping them on the window or Dock icon, or with ⌘O.
  They play next and can join playlists.
- **A song that can't play is skipped,** and the app says why: damaged, a kind of file macOS can't decode, or not
  found. A FLAC that macOS can't decode used to crash the app; it no longer does.

**Your library**
- **⌘F searches your library from anywhere,** with one rule on every tab. Sinhala, other scripts and decomposed text
  are found, and so is a single syllable.
- **"Use this drive":** when you move your music to another drive, point the paused folder at it. Your songs keep
  their history. Songs not on the new drive are removed, and the folder is left alone if none are found.
- **"Remove Missing…"** never removes songs whose drive is only unplugged.

## Known issues
The open list is in [known-issues.md](https://github.com/ramith/sound-engineering/blob/main/docs/product/known-issues.md).
Notable for this alpha:
- **Not notarized** (see Install).
- **Pure Mode on Bluetooth** plays in Enhanced: Bluetooth re-encodes the audio, so it can't be bit-perfect.
- **The first play of a song that isn't measured yet is quieter:** about 5 dB, and up to about 10 dB for a surround
  track on Bluetooth. From its next play it is exact.
- **The LFE level in a surround fold** (−6 dB per side when the LFE is included) hasn't been tuned by ear yet. None of
  the test music carries an LFE of its own.
- **Switching from a surround device to stereo in the middle of a surround track** isn't folded again until the next
  track.
- **Seamless track changes:** in some cases the next song starts about 0.2 s late.
