**AdaptiveSound 1.0.0-alpha** is the first release (R1), a music player for your own library on macOS. It is an
early build: expect rough edges, and please report what you find.

## Requirements
- macOS 26 (Tahoe) on Apple Silicon.
- Optional: [FFmpeg](https://formulae.brew.sh/formula/ffmpeg) via Homebrew (`brew install ffmpeg`) for formats
  Apple's decoders don't cover. Without it, the app uses Apple's decoders.

## Install
1. Download `AdaptiveSound-1.0.0-alpha-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag **AdaptiveSound** to **Applications**.
2. This alpha is **not notarized by Apple yet**, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open **System Settings ›
   Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to AdaptiveSound. You only do this once.
   - Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AdaptiveSound.app`

## Verify the download
These files were built by GitHub Actions from this release's tag, not on a personal machine, and each one carries a
signed build-provenance attestation (Sigstore). To check one:
```
gh attestation verify AdaptiveSound-1.0.0-alpha-macos-arm64.dmg --repo ramith/sound-engineering
```
`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of each file.

## What's in it
- **Your library:** add music folders and browse Songs, Albums, Artists and Genres, with album, artist and genre pages,
  filtering and sorting. Changes on disk are picked up live, and at the next launch.
- **Playlists:** create, rename and reorder; drag songs onto a playlist.
- **Now Playing:** a queue with Up Next and Recent, shuffle and repeat, gapless playback, a live spectrum analyzer,
  and loudness readouts.
- **EQ:** a 31-point graphic EQ that plays what it draws, with Tone controls (Bass, Body, Mids, Presence, Air), built-in
  presets and your own, a hold-to-compare, and per-output memory. It stays at the same loudness, with a safety limiter.
- **Pure Mode:** bit-perfect output straight to your device, the device following each track's sample rate (no EQ or
  effects). A track it can't play exactly (for example a mono track on stereo speakers) plays in Enhanced,
  and the app says so.
- **Output devices:** the app follows the system output until you pick a device; then it stays there, and returns to
  it when it reconnects. Unplugging pauses playback and keeps your place.
- **Monitoring:** per-channel before / after spectrum, up to 7.1.
- **Design:** a Liquid Glass look in dark and light, VoiceOver and full-keyboard support.

## Known issues
The open list is in [known-issues.md](https://github.com/ramith/sound-engineering/blob/main/docs/product/known-issues.md).
Notable for this alpha:
- **Not notarized** (see Install).
- **Pure Mode on Bluetooth** falls back to Enhanced.
- **Seamless track changes:** a mid-song Repeat change, and the 48 kHz path, can leave a tiny gap between tracks.
- **VoiceOver** keeps the app fairly busy while a song plays.
- **A replaced music drive:** if you move your music to a new drive with the same name, the app pauses that folder to
  protect your library. Don't remove and re-add the folder: that resets play counts, loved and ratings for songs not
  in a playlist. A fix is coming.
- **Your output device choice** is remembered until you quit, not across launches.
